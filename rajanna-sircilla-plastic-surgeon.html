Plastic Surgeon for Rajanna Sircilla Patients

This is the district guide — for Sircilla town itself, see our Sircilla town guide. Rajanna Sircilla district is Telangana's textile heartland, and textile work is hand work: powerloom injuries, repetitive strain, cuts. The district also needs reconstructive and cosmetic expertise. AR Plastic Surgery in Karimnagar covers it all.

A textile district needs a hand surgeon

Powerlooms don't forgive inattention — and when they injure a hand, the difference between a dressing and a specialist decides the outcome. Dr Ashok Reddy — MBBS, DNB (Mumbai), M.Ch (Delhi), Gold Medalist — trained hand & microvascular surgeon:

  • Hand trauma surgery — loom and machinery injuries, urgently assessed.
  • Tendon repair — restoring movement after cuts.
  • Carpal tunnel release — for chronic numbness from repetitive work.
  • Scar revision — after healed workplace injuries.

Beyond the workplace

Burn reconstruction, cleft repair — and cosmetic procedures (hair transplant, gynecomastia, liposuction) with confidential consultations. Sircilla town residents: see the town guide.
