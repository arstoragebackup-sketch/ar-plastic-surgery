Plastic Surgeon for Warangal Patients

Warangal has no shortage of hospitals — but a dedicated plastic surgery practice, where one gold-medalist surgeon plans and performs your procedure from first consultation to aftercare, is harder to find. That depth of specialist care is why patients from Warangal make the trip to AR Plastic Surgery in Karimnagar.

Why the trip from Warangal is worth it

Led by Dr Ashok Reddy — MBBS, DNB (Mumbai), M.Ch (Delhi), Gold Medalist, plastic, cosmetic, hand & microvascular surgeon, the clinic offers something general hospitals rarely match: complete continuity, with the same surgeon at every step.

  • Hand & microvascular surgery — tendon repair, nerve repair, microsurgical reconstruction and hand trauma care under magnification.
  • Surgeon-led cosmetic procedures — FUE hair transplant, gynecomastia surgery, rhinoplasty and liposuction, planned and performed by Dr Reddy himself — never delegated.
  • Reconstructive expertise — burn reconstruction, scar revision and cleft lip & palate repair.
  • Non-surgical aesthetics — Botox, fillers, PRP and lasers, doctor-administered.
