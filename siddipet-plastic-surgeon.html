Plastic Surgeon for Siddipet Patients

Siddipet has good skin and hair clinics — but some treatments need a full-scope plastic surgeon, not a skin clinic. For hand surgery, cleft repair and burn reconstruction, our October 2026 review of local listings found no dedicated local provider; the nearest full-scope option is AR Plastic Surgery in Karimnagar.

Care that is hard to find locally in Siddipet

Led by Dr Ashok Reddy — MBBS, DNB (Mumbai), M.Ch (Delhi), Gold Medalist, plastic, cosmetic, hand & microvascular surgeon, the clinic covers the procedures local listings rarely offer:

  • Hand surgery & microvascular repair — tendon injuries, fractures, nerve repair and microsurgical reconstruction.
  • Cleft lip & palate repair — staged surgical correction, planned from the earliest months of life.
  • Burn reconstruction — contracture release and staged rebuilding after burns have healed.
  • Scar revision, skin grafting, facial trauma — reconstructive procedures that need a plastic surgeon's planning.

Surgeon-led cosmetic care

For hair transplant, gynecomastia surgery, liposuction and rhinoplasty, the difference at AR is that every case is planned and performed by a qualified plastic surgeon — not delegated. Consultations are confidential. Free OP consultations are available every Wednesday.

Many Siddipet patients combine their consultation with a single day trip. Call or book online to reserve your slot.
