Hand Surgeon in Karimnagar

Meet your hand surgeon

Dr Ashok Reddy — MBBS, DNB (Mumbai), M.Ch (Delhi), Gold Medalist — trained at premier institutions (DNB, Mumbai; M.Ch, Delhi, Gold Medal) and practises hand & microvascular surgery at AR Plastic Surgery, Karimnagar. His hand practice covers the full spectrum: emergency trauma, tendon and nerve repair under the operating microscope, fingertip replantation, and planned surgery for conditions like carpal tunnel syndrome and trigger finger.

What a hand surgeon treats

  • Hand trauma — fractures, crush injuries, deep lacerations, amputations
  • Tendon repair — divided flexor and extensor tendons
  • Nerve repair & microsurgery — injured nerves, microsurgical reconstruction
  • Carpal tunnel release — numbness, tingling, night pain in the hand
  • Fingertip replantation — microsurgical reattachment after amputation

Why a plastic surgeon for hand surgery?

Hand surgery demands microsurgery: joining vessels and nerves barely a millimetre wide under an operating microscope, with sutures finer than a human hair. This is core plastic-surgical training. A hand surgeon with this background can assess the whole injured hand — bone, tendon, nerve, vessel and skin — as one system rather than in parts.

Common questions

Hand surgeon FAQs

Who is a hand surgeon?

A hand surgeon is a specialist who treats injuries and conditions of the hand, wrist and forearm — bones, tendons, nerves, vessels and skin. In India, hand surgeons usually come from plastic surgery or orthopaedic backgrounds with additional super-specialty training in hand and microsurgery.

Why see a plastic surgeon for a hand problem?

Plastic surgeons with hand training bring microsurgery — operating under magnification on structures millimetres wide. Tendon, nerve and vessel repair demands this precision. Dr Ashok Reddy is an M.Ch plastic surgeon and Gold Medalist with dedicated hand & microvascular training.

What conditions does a hand surgeon treat?

Hand fractures, deep cuts, crush injuries, fingertip amputations, divided tendons and nerves, carpal tunnel syndrome, trigger finger, and post-injury stiffness. Both emergency trauma and planned treatment for ongoing hand problems.

Do I need to travel to Hyderabad for hand surgery?

No. AR Plastic Surgery in Karimnagar provides full-time hand surgical care — emergency assessment, microsurgical repair and hand therapy — so patients across Telangana can be treated close to home.
