Plastic Surgeon for Khammam Patients

From Khammam in southern Telangana, specialist plastic surgery has traditionally meant a long journey to Hyderabad. AR Plastic Surgery in Karimnagar offers a closer full-scope alternative — reconstructive, cosmetic and hand surgery under one roof, led by a gold-medalist surgeon.

Cleft, burns and scar care for Khammam families

Some of the most life-changing plastic surgery is reconstructive. Dr Ashok Reddy — MBBS, DNB (Mumbai), M.Ch (Delhi), Gold Medalist — provides:

  • Cleft lip & palate repair — staged surgical correction from the earliest months of life, as part of long-term care.
  • Burn reconstruction — releasing tight scars and rebuilding form and movement, step by step.
  • Scar revision — making prominent or restrictive scars less noticeable.
  • Hand surgery — tendon, nerve and fracture care by a trained microvascular surgeon.

Confidential cosmetic consultations

Concerns like gynecomastia, hair loss (FUE transplant), body contour (liposuction, tummy tuck) or nose shape (rhinoplasty) are discussed in fully private consultations — with honest guidance on whether surgery is even the right answer.
