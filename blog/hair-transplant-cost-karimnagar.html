How hair transplant pricing works

Almost every clinic prices a transplant the same way: number of grafts × per-graft rate, plus the surgeon's fee and clinic costs. A graft is a tiny unit of 1–4 hair follicles taken from your donor area (usually the back of the scalp) and implanted where hair is thinning.

In the Karimnagar–Hyderabad region, FUE (follicular unit extraction — the technique where grafts are extracted one by one, leaving no linear scar) is typically priced at roughly ₹35–₹60 per graft. So the biggest driver of your total is simply how much area needs covering.

6 factors that decide your price

  1. Number of grafts. The single biggest factor — more area to cover means more grafts.
  2. Technique. FUE is the standard. Variants like DHI (direct implantation) typically cost more per graft because they are slower and more instrument-intensive.
  3. Who performs the surgery. A qualified plastic surgeon designing the hairline and performing the procedure costs more than a technician-led setup — and that difference matters for safety and planning.
  4. Donor characteristics. Fine hair or limited donor density can mean more grafts are needed for the same coverage.
  5. Sessions. Extensive baldness sometimes needs two sittings spaced months apart, which raises the total.
  6. Add-ons. PRP therapy (indicatively ₹5,000–₹12,000 per session), medications, and aftercare kits may or may not be bundled — always ask.

What's usually included — and what to ask

A proper written quote should itemise: the consultation and scalp mapping, the procedure itself, anaesthesia, the surgical team's fee, post-procedure medications or kit, and scheduled follow-up reviews. Before you commit at any clinic, ask:

  • Who will perform the surgery — the surgeon or a technician?
  • Exactly how many grafts are planned, and how was that counted?
  • Is PRP or aftercare included, or billed separately?
  • What happens if a second session is needed later?
  • Can I see the quote in writing before the procedure day?

Red flags in very low quotes

If a quote is far below the market range, slow down and check: is a qualified surgeon actually performing the procedure, or is it delegated entirely to technicians? Are graft numbers realistic for your grade of loss, or inflated to look like a bargain per graft? Is aftercare included at all? Price matters, but a transplant is surgery — the cheapest option is not automatically the safest one.

Common questions

What is the per-graft cost of FUE in Karimnagar?

Roughly ₹35–₹60 per graft across the region. Your total is grafts × rate, so a 1,500-graft procedure indicatively lands around ₹55,000–₹90,000.

How many grafts will I need?

Early recession: 500–1,000. Moderate loss: 1,000–1,800. Advanced: 1,800–2,800. Extensive baldness: 2,800+, sometimes over two sessions. Donor density also plays a role.

Is PRP included in the cost?

Not always — some clinics bundle it, others charge per session (indicatively ₹5,000–₹12,000). Ask for an itemised quote.

Why do prices vary so much between clinics?

Graft count, technique, who performs the surgery, and what's bundled (PRP, aftercare, follow-ups) all move the number. Compare written, itemised quotes — not advertisements.

How do I get my exact quote?

One consultation: the surgeon examines your scalp, maps the area, checks donor density, and gives you a written figure. That's the only quote that actually applies to you.
