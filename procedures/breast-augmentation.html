Breast Augmentation in Karimnagar

Whether the goal is restoring volume lost over time or achieving better proportion, breast augmentation is a deeply personal decision. At AR Plastic Surgery, Dr Ashok Reddy guides you through every option with unhurried, honest counsel.

Overview

Breast augmentation increases breast size and fullness using implants placed beneath the breast tissue or chest muscle. It can enhance naturally small breasts, restore volume lost after pregnancy or weight change, and improve asymmetry between the two sides. Implant choices — including fill material, shape, profile, and size — are discussed in detail, because each option suits different bodies and goals. Your chest width, existing tissue, and lifestyle all influence which options are sensible for you.

The procedure suits healthy adults who have thought carefully about why they want change and hold realistic expectations. Common motivations include feeling out of proportion, difficulty finding clothes that fit, or wanting to restore the pre-pregnancy figure. Candidates should be at a stable weight, in good general health, and free from untreated breast conditions. A clear, personal motivation matters more than anyone else's opinion. The decision should feel settled and personal, never rushed or driven by someone else's preferences.

Consultation covers your goals, body proportions, breast tissue, and chest dimensions to identify suitable implant options. Dr Reddy reviews your medical and family history, including any breast screening, and discusses how pregnancy, weight change, and ageing may affect the result over time. If your expectations cannot be met safely, or if a lift rather than augmentation is what you need, he will redirect the plan honestly. Previous breast surgery, breastfeeding history, and screening results are all reviewed as part of this planning.

Honest expectations include understanding that implants are long-term but not lifetime devices: future surgery is likely at some point, whether for replacement, repositioning, or removal. Breasts will continue to change with age, weight, and pregnancy. Scars are placed discreetly but remain visible on close inspection. The aim is a fuller contour that suits your frame — not a particular cup size — and results vary from person to person. Ongoing follow-up visits help monitor implant health over the years.

Our approach

Dr Reddy approaches augmentation as proportion work, not size work. Measurements of your chest, breast base, and tissue guide implant selection so the result harmonises with your body rather than overwhelming it. Pocket creation and implant positioning are performed with careful attention to symmetry and a natural slope, and every incision is planned for the most discreet placement your anatomy allows.

Counsel defines the experience as much as surgery. Dr Reddy spends consultation time making sure the decision is yours, unhurried and fully informed — including the long-term realities of implants. He will decline to place implants that are disproportionate to your frame or driven by someone else's wishes. Sterile technique and careful tissue handling support safe healing, with structured follow-up afterward.

What to expect

Consultation

You will talk through your motivations, desired change, and lifestyle — including plans for future pregnancy. Dr Reddy examines your breasts and chest, takes measurements, and discusses implant options suited to your body. You will see how different choices affect proportion, learn where scars will sit, and hear honestly about longevity, risks, and alternatives. The decision is never rushed; second visits are welcome.

The procedure

Breast augmentation is performed under general anaesthesia. Through a discreet incision, a pocket is created and the chosen implant is positioned, with care taken to achieve symmetry and a natural contour. The incisions are closed in layers to support fine healing. The procedure typically takes a couple of hours, and you are monitored as you wake before going home with aftercare instructions.

Recovery

Soreness, tightness, and swelling are expected for the first days and ease gradually. A supportive garment is worn as directed, and arm movements are limited initially to protect healing tissues. Most patients return to desk routines within a week or so, with exercise reintroduced in stages at follow-up. Implants settle into position over weeks to months, which is when the shape is properly judged.

Risks and limitations — stated honestly

Every procedure carries trade-offs. Before you decide, Dr Ashok Reddy will discuss these with you directly:

  • Capsular contracture: scar tissue naturally forms around every implant, but in some patients it tightens and hardens, distorting shape and causing discomfort. Further surgery may be needed to address it.
  • Implant rupture or leakage: implants can rupture over time, sometimes silently. Monitoring and eventual replacement are part of responsible long-term implant ownership, discussed openly at consultation.
  • Changes in sensation: nipple or breast sensation may change after surgery — heightened, reduced, or numb — and while this often improves, some change can be lasting.
  • Need for future surgery: implants are not lifetime devices, and factors like ageing, weight change, or personal preference may lead to revision, replacement, or removal later in life.

FAQ

Common questions

What is the difference between silicone and saline implants?

Both have a silicone shell; silicone implants are filled with cohesive gel while saline implants are filled with sterile salt water. Many patients and surgeons feel silicone gel gives a more natural feel, while saline allows smaller incisions and makes rupture obvious. Dr Reddy explains the trade-offs of each and recommends based on your tissue, goals, and preferences.

How long do breast implants last?

Implants are long-term devices but not lifetime ones. Many last a decade or more, but replacement, repositioning, or removal is likely at some point due to rupture, contracture, changing taste, or body changes. Planning for this reality — rather than assuming one operation lasts forever — is part of the honest counselling Dr Reddy provides.

Will I be able to breastfeed after augmentation?

Many women breastfeed successfully after augmentation, especially when incisions avoid the areola and implants sit beneath the muscle. However, no surgeon can promise unaffected breastfeeding, since milk production depends on many factors. If future breastfeeding is important to you, discuss it specifically at consultation so the plan respects it.

How do mammograms work with implants?

Breast screening remains important and is absolutely possible with implants, though special displacement views are used and you should always inform the imaging centre beforehand. Implants do not prevent cancer detection, but they add a step to the process. Dr Reddy discusses ongoing breast health as part of long-term planning.

How do I choose the right size?

The goal is proportion, not a number. Dr Reddy measures your chest and breast dimensions and discusses how different volumes would look on your frame, considering your height, shoulders, and lifestyle. Trying sizers during consultation helps translate abstract volumes into a visual sense. He will caution against sizes that would look or feel disproportionate.
