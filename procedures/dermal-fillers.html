Dermal Fillers in Karimnagar

Volume loss in the cheeks, lips, and under-eyes is one of the earliest visible signs of ageing. Dermal fillers at AR Plastic Surgery restore subtle volume and contour with careful, conservative placement by Dr Ashok Reddy.

Overview

Dermal fillers are gel-based substances injected beneath the skin to restore lost volume, smooth folds, and refine contours. Most modern fillers use hyaluronic acid — a substance found naturally in the skin — and can enhance cheeks, lips, the under-eye region, nasolabial folds, and the jawline. The effect is immediate, though initial swelling means the settled result is judged after a week or two. Treatment is highly customisable, with different products chosen for structure versus softness.

Fillers suit adults noticing volume loss, asymmetry, or thinning lips who want improvement without surgery. They work well for age-related hollowing and for defining features like the jawline or chin. Candidates should be in good health, have realistic expectations about subtle enhancement, and understand that fillers are temporary and need maintenance. They are also used to refine the chin, temples, and jawline for better overall facial balance. First-time patients often start with a modest amount and build gradually.

At consultation Dr Reddy assesses your facial structure, skin quality, and the specific areas of volume loss, and distinguishes concerns suited to fillers from those needing Botox, skin treatments, or surgery. He reviews your medical history, including allergies and previous treatments. If the change you want exceeds what fillers can deliver naturally, or if your features need no enhancement, he will advise against treatment. Previous filler treatments elsewhere are reviewed carefully, since old product affects new planning.

Honest expectations: fillers provide enhancement, not transformation, and overfilling looks worse than no treatment at all. The effect lasts months to over a year depending on the product and area, then gradually fades. Bruising and swelling are common initially. Hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved if necessary, which is a meaningful safety advantage. Drinking water and avoiding excessive heat in the first days supports comfortable healing. Results vary from person to person.

Our approach

Dr Reddy injects conservatively and symmetrically, treating the face as a whole rather than isolated lines — because filling one fold without addressing surrounding volume can look odd. He chooses products suited to each area's needs: firmer gels for structure, softer ones for delicate zones. Every placement is deliberate, with the aim that no one can tell treatment was done.

Safety is paramount with fillers, since rare but serious vascular complications exist. Dr Reddy uses careful technique, understands facial vascular anatomy, and keeps reversal agents available. He will decline treatment when the anatomy is unfavourable, when expectations demand overfilling, or when a surgical option would serve better — and he explains his reasoning plainly.

What to expect

Consultation

You will discuss which areas concern you — cheeks, lips, under-eyes, jawline — and what kind of change feels right. Dr Reddy examines your facial proportions and volume distribution, explains which products suit each area, and shows honestly what improvement to expect. Your medical history is reviewed, risks are explained, and there is no pressure to proceed. Photographs taken at consultation help track subtle changes objectively over time.

The procedure

After cleansing and optional numbing, filler is injected with a fine needle or blunt cannula into the planned areas — a process taking minutes to half an hour depending on extent. You can see the change immediately and give feedback during treatment. Ice and gentle pressure manage any pinpoint bleeding, and you leave with aftercare guidance. The whole appointment usually takes less than an hour, including consultation and numbing time.

Recovery

Swelling, redness, and occasional bruising are expected for several days; the settled result is judged after one to two weeks. Avoiding strenuous exercise, heat, and pressure on treated areas for a day or two helps. Dr Reddy reviews the result with you, and small refinements can be made once swelling has fully resolved. Most people return to normal routines the next day, with any bruising easily covered if needed.

Risks and limitations — stated honestly

Every procedure carries trade-offs. Before you decide, Dr Ashok Reddy will discuss these with you directly:

  • Bruising and swelling: these are common and usually settle within days to two weeks. Under-eye and lip areas bruise more readily, which is discussed beforehand.
  • Lumps and asymmetry: filler can occasionally feel lumpy or sit unevenly. Gentle massage or a small adjustment usually resolves this once initial swelling settles.
  • Vascular occlusion: rarely, filler can block a blood vessel, which is a serious complication needing urgent treatment. Choosing an experienced doctor who keeps reversal agents available is the key safeguard.
  • Infection and allergic reaction: uncommon with modern hyaluronic acid fillers, but possible with any injection. Sterile technique and proper product sourcing minimise these risks.

FAQ

Common questions

What are dermal fillers made of?

Most modern fillers use hyaluronic acid, a gel-like substance found naturally in skin that attracts water and adds volume. It is biocompatible and gradually broken down by the body. Because it occurs naturally, allergic reactions are uncommon — and importantly, hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved if ever needed.

How long do fillers last?

It depends on the product, the area treated, and your metabolism — generally months to over a year, with lips tending to fade sooner than cheeks. The effect diminishes gradually rather than disappearing overnight. Dr Reddy discusses realistic duration for your specific treatment plan and spaces maintenance sensibly.

Can fillers be reversed?

Hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved with an enzyme injection, which is a significant safety advantage — if the result is unsatisfactory or a complication occurs, it can be undone. This reversibility is one reason Dr Reddy prefers hyaluronic acid products, and it is discussed at every consultation.

Will my lips look natural?

They should — the overdone look comes from excessive volume, not from fillers themselves. Dr Reddy enhances lip shape and proportion conservatively, respecting your natural anatomy. During treatment you can see the change developing and give feedback, so the result stays within the natural-looking range you discussed.

What is the difference between fillers and Botox?

They treat different problems: Botox relaxes muscles to soften dynamic wrinkles caused by expression, while fillers restore volume lost to ageing. Frown lines may need Botox; hollow cheeks need fillers; many faces benefit from a thoughtful combination. Dr Reddy assesses which — or whether both — suits each area of your face.
