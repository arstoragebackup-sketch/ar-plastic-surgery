Eyelid Surgery (Blepharoplasty) in Karimnagar

Heavy eyelids and under-eye bags can make you look tired even when you are not. Blepharoplasty at AR Plastic Surgery removes excess eyelid skin and fat with fine, discreetly placed incisions for a brighter, more rested appearance.

Overview

Blepharoplasty is surgery of the eyelids. Upper blepharoplasty removes excess skin — and sometimes fat — that hoods the eyes, while lower blepharoplasty addresses puffiness and bags beneath the eyes, often by repositioning fat rather than simply removing it. In some cases, drooping upper lids obstruct the upper field of vision, giving the surgery a functional as well as aesthetic purpose. Careful, conservative removal is the rule, since in eyelid surgery taking too much is far harder to correct.

It suits adults bothered by hooded upper lids, persistent under-eye bags, or a tired appearance that rest does not fix. Candidates should have good general eye health; conditions such as dry eye, thyroid eye disease, or retinal problems need evaluation first. Realistic expectations — brighter and rested, not a different face — and good general health complete the picture. People who look tired despite sleeping well are often the most pleased with the change.

Consultation includes examination of eyelid skin, fat, muscle tone, and eye health, plus review of your medical and vision history. Dr Reddy assesses whether skin, fat, or both cause your concern, and whether brow position contributes — sometimes a heavy brow, not the lids, is the real issue. If surgery would not meaningfully help, or if dry eye makes it risky, he will say so. Photographs and measurements taken at consultation guide precise, symmetrical planning.

Honest expectations: scars hide in the natural eyelid crease or inside the lower lid and fade well, but healing takes weeks and final refinement months. Dark circles caused by pigmentation rather than shadowing may persist. The eyes will look more open and rested, not decades younger overnight. Keeping the eyes lubricated and protected from sun during healing supports the finest outcome. As with any surgery, results vary from person to person.

Our approach

Dr Reddy plans conservatively — in eyelid surgery, removing too much is far worse than removing too little. Markings are made with you awake and looking straight ahead, since lid position changes when you lie down. Fat is repositioned where possible to avoid a hollowed look, and closure uses fine sutures for the least visible scars.

Eye safety governs every decision. He evaluates tear function and lid closure before operating and will decline surgery when the risk to eye comfort or vision outweighs the benefit. The procedure is performed with meticulous technique under appropriate anaesthesia, and aftercare — cold compresses, ointment, sun protection — is explained in detail, because gentle early care shapes the final result.

What to expect

Consultation

You will describe what bothers you — heaviness, bags, tired look — and Dr Reddy will examine your lids, brow position, and eye health. Photographs document the starting point. He explains whether upper lids, lower lids, or both need attention, where scars will sit, and what improvement is realistic. Your vision history and dry-eye symptoms are reviewed carefully before any plan is made.

The procedure

Blepharoplasty is usually performed under local anaesthesia with sedation. For upper lids, excess skin and fat are removed through an incision hidden in the natural crease; for lower lids, the approach may be through the inside of the lid, leaving no external scar. Fine sutures close the incisions. The procedure typically takes one to two hours. You will rest with cool compresses afterward before going home the same day with detailed aftercare.

Recovery

Swelling and bruising around the eyes are expected and look worse than they feel, settling over one to two weeks. Cold compresses, head elevation, and prescribed ointment support healing. Stitches are removed within days. Light sensitivity and watery eyes are common early on. Final refinement continues for months, and sun protection helps scars fade as well as possible. Most patients feel comfortable returning to social activities within two weeks.

Risks and limitations — stated honestly

Every procedure carries trade-offs. Before you decide, Dr Ashok Reddy will discuss these with you directly:

  • Dry eyes and irritation: eyelid surgery can temporarily worsen dryness or cause a gritty sensation. Pre-existing dry eye is evaluated beforehand, and lubricants are part of aftercare.
  • Difficulty closing the eyes: removing too much skin can impair lid closure, which is why conservative planning matters. Temporary tightness is common; lasting problems are uncommon with careful technique.
  • Asymmetry: the two eyes are never perfectly identical, and minor differences in healing can occur. Significant asymmetry is uncommon but is discussed as a possibility.
  • Visible scarring: upper-lid scars usually hide well in the crease, but individual healing varies, and lower-lid incisions can occasionally heal visibly. Scar maturation takes months.

FAQ

Common questions

What is the difference between upper and lower blepharoplasty?

Upper blepharoplasty removes hooding skin (and sometimes fat) from the upper lids, often improving both appearance and vision. Lower blepharoplasty addresses bags and puffiness beneath the eyes, frequently by repositioning fat to smooth the lid-cheek junction. Many patients need only one; some benefit from both. Dr Reddy assesses each area separately at consultation.

Will blepharoplasty fix my dark circles?

It depends on their cause. Circles caused by shadowing from bags or hollows often improve when the contour is smoothed. But pigmentation — actual dark colouring of the skin — is not corrected by surgery and needs medical or laser treatment instead. Dr Reddy examines the cause honestly and will not promise improvement surgery cannot deliver.

Is eyelid surgery painful?

Most patients are surprised by how manageable it is. The procedure itself is done under anaesthesia, and afterward there is typically tightness, mild soreness, and watering rather than significant pain. Simple measures — cold compresses, head elevation, prescribed ointment — handle the early days comfortably for most people.

How long do the results last?

Upper-lid results are long-lasting, though ageing continues and skin will gradually relax again over years. Lower-lid improvement also endures well, since repositioned fat does not simply return. No procedure stops ageing, but blepharoplasty resets the starting point. Dr Reddy discusses realistic longevity at consultation.

Will people notice I had surgery?

The aim is that they notice you look rested, not operated. Because incisions hide in natural creases and the change is one of refreshment rather than transformation, most patients find colleagues comment on their rested appearance without guessing why. Conservative planning is what keeps the result discreet.
