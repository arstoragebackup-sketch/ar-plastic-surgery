Chemical Peels in Karimnagar

Pigmentation, acne marks, and dull, uneven skin often sit too deep for creams to reach. Chemical peels at AR Plastic Surgery offer controlled exfoliation — matched to your skin type and concern — under Dr Ashok Reddy's supervision.

Overview

A chemical peel applies a carefully chosen solution to the skin to remove its damaged outer layers in a controlled way, encouraging fresher, more even skin to emerge. Peels range from superficial to deeper, and are used for pigmentation including melasma, acne marks, fine lines, rough texture, and dullness. The choice of peel depth is matched to the concern being treated and the patient's skin. Mild peels need no downtime, while stronger ones involve several days of visible peeling.

Peels suit adults troubled by uneven tone, pigmentation, post-acne marks, or early fine lines who want gradual, natural improvement. They are often part of a broader skin plan rather than a standalone fix. A crucial consideration in Indian skin is the risk of post-inflammatory pigmentation — darker skin types need conservative peel selection, which is why medical supervision matters. Peels are often combined with medical skincare and strict sun protection for the most reliable improvement.

At consultation Dr Reddy examines your skin type, the nature of your pigmentation or marks, your sun exposure, and your history of pigmentation problems. He selects the peel type and strength accordingly — or advises against peels if your skin is unsuitable or if another treatment would work better. Aggressive peeling of unsuitable skin causes more harm than good, and he will not risk it. Your current skincare products are reviewed too, since some interact with peeling agents.

Honest expectations: improvement is gradual across a series of sessions, not dramatic after one. Peeling and redness for several days follow each treatment, and strict sun protection is non-negotiable — without it, pigmentation returns. Peels improve tone and texture; they do not change skin colour or erase deep scars. Consistency matters more than intensity: a well-planned series outperforms a single aggressive peel. Patience through the series is rewarded with steadier, safer improvement. Results vary from person to person.

Our approach

Dr Reddy's approach is skin-type-first: the peel is chosen for your pigmentation risk as much as for your concern, favouring conservative, repeatable treatments over aggressive single sessions. Skin is prepared beforehand when needed, the peel is applied with careful timing and neutralisation, and aftercare — moisturisation, sun protection, and what to avoid — is explained in detail.

Candour about limits defines the practice. He will tell you when pigmentation is hormonal or deep enough that peels can only modestly help, when sun discipline matters more than any procedure, and when to stop a series that is not delivering. Progress is reviewed at each visit, and the plan adapts to how your skin actually responds.

What to expect

Consultation

You will discuss your skin concerns — pigmentation, marks, texture — and your routine, sun exposure, and previous treatments. Dr Reddy examines your skin under good light, identifies the type and depth of the problem, and explains which peel, if any, suits you. You will learn what each session involves, how many are typically needed, and what aftercare demands. You will also learn how to prepare your skin in the days before the first session.

The procedure

The skin is cleansed and prepared, then the peel solution is applied evenly and left for a precisely timed period before being neutralised or removed. You will feel tingling, warmth, or stinging depending on the peel's strength — uncomfortable but brief. The session takes under an hour, and soothing care is applied before you leave with aftercare instructions. A neutralising step stops the peel precisely on time for your skin type.

Recovery

Redness and tightness follow, with visible peeling over several days for most peels — this is the treatment working, not a complication. Picking at peeling skin is discouraged. Sun protection is essential throughout the series. Makeup is usually avoided for a day or two. Each session's recovery is similar, and improvement accumulates across the series. Between sessions, gentle skincare and diligent sun protection keep progress on track.

Risks and limitations — stated honestly

Every procedure carries trade-offs. Before you decide, Dr Ashok Reddy will discuss these with you directly:

  • Redness and peeling: expected effects that can look dramatic for a few days. While normal, the downtime should be planned around work and social commitments.
  • Pigmentation changes: peels can cause darkening or lightening, particularly in darker skin types or with sun exposure during healing. Conservative peel choice and sun discipline minimise this.
  • Scarring: uncommon with superficial peels but a real risk with deeper ones or if healing is disturbed. This is why peel depth is matched carefully to skin type.
  • Infection and cold sores: the treated skin is vulnerable while healing, and peels can trigger cold sore outbreaks in susceptible people. Preventive measures are discussed when relevant.

FAQ

Common questions

Which chemical peel is right for me?

It depends on your concern and your skin type — there is no universally ideal peel. Superficial peels suit mild unevenness with minimal downtime; stronger peels address deeper pigmentation but need more recovery and carry more risk. Dr Reddy examines your skin and selects conservatively, because the wrong peel on the wrong skin causes more harm than good.

How many peel sessions will I need?

Peels work as a series, not a single event — improvement accumulates gradually across sessions spaced weeks apart. The number depends on your concern and how your skin responds. Dr Reddy reviews progress at each visit and adjusts the plan, stopping the series when your skin has improved as much as peels reasonably can.

Is there downtime after a chemical peel?

Yes — expect redness, tightness, and visible peeling for several days after most peels, varying with the peel's strength. This is the treatment working. You should plan sessions around work and events, avoid picking at peeling skin, and protect healing skin from the sun rigorously.

Are chemical peels safe for Indian skin?

They can be, with the right peel and proper supervision — but darker skin carries a higher risk of post-inflammatory pigmentation, so aggressive peeling is avoided. Dr Reddy selects conservative peels suited to your skin type, prepares the skin when needed, and insists on sun protection. This caution is exactly why medical supervision matters.

Should I choose a peel or laser treatment?

It depends on the concern: peels excel at superficial pigmentation, texture, and acne marks with lower cost and simpler aftercare, while lasers target deeper pigmentation, vascular issues, or hair. Sometimes they complement each other. Dr Reddy assesses your skin and recommends honestly — including when neither is the right answer.
