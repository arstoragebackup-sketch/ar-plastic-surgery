Liposuction in Karimnagar

Some pockets of fat simply do not respond to diet and exercise. Liposuction at AR Plastic Surgery is a body-contouring procedure that removes localised fat deposits to improve shape and proportion — planned carefully, never sold as a shortcut.

Overview

Liposuction removes localised deposits of fat through small incisions using a thin tube called a cannula. It is a contouring operation, not a weight-loss method: its purpose is to reshape areas such as the abdomen, flanks, thighs, upper arms, back, or under the chin where fat persists despite a healthy lifestyle. The technique aims to leave smooth transitions between treated and untreated areas so the change looks natural. Common treatment zones are planned together so the overall silhouette stays balanced rather than over-treated in one spot.

The procedure suits adults at or near a stable, healthy weight who have distinct pockets of fat and reasonably firm, elastic skin. It works well as a refining step for people already living an active lifestyle, rather than as a starting point for major weight change. Many patients choose it after reaching their target weight but finding that certain areas still hold disproportionate fullness. Patients should be non-smokers or willing to stop, in good general health, and clear that liposuction reshapes rather than transforms the whole body.

Candidacy is assessed at consultation, where Dr Reddy examines skin quality, fat distribution, and overall health. Liposuction is a poor choice for generalised obesity, for skin that hangs loosely (which may need excision surgery instead), or for patients expecting dramatic weight loss. If your goals would be better served by weight management first, or by a procedure like a tummy tuck, he will redirect you honestly rather than operate. Body mass trends, medication history, and previous surgeries are all part of this assessment.

Honest expectations protect you from disappointment. Swelling after surgery masks the result for weeks, and the final contour emerges gradually over months. Skin retraction varies: younger, elastic skin adapts better, while looser skin may show some laxity after fat removal. Treated fat cells are removed, but remaining cells can still enlarge with weight gain, so a stable lifestyle preserves the improvement. Wearing the compression garment as directed and staying gently active in the early weeks supports smoother healing. Results vary from person to person.

Our approach

Dr Reddy marks the treatment areas with you standing, since gravity changes how fat distributes, and plans conservative, symmetrical removal rather than aggressive extraction. During surgery, careful technique aims for even contouring and preservation of a smooth layer beneath the skin. The focus is on proportion — how each area relates to the rest of the body — not on the volume of fat removed.

Safety governs every decision, including how much can be treated in one session. Dr Reddy will decline or stage the procedure if your goals exceed safe limits, and he will tell you plainly when loose skin means liposuction alone would disappoint. Sterile technique, careful fluid management, and attentive aftercare with compression garments are standard, and follow-up visits track your healing.

What to expect

Consultation

You will discuss which areas bother you, your weight history, and what you hope to change. Dr Reddy examines skin elasticity and fat distribution, pinching and marking areas to show what liposuction can and cannot improve. Your medical history and medications are reviewed for surgical safety. You will hear realistic possibilities, the limits of the procedure, and what recovery involves — with honest guidance if another approach suits you better.

The procedure

Liposuction is performed under anaesthesia chosen for your case and the extent of treatment. Small incisions admit the cannula, and fat is removed in controlled passes designed to leave smooth, even contours. Treated areas are dressed and a compression garment is fitted before you leave. The procedure length depends on how many areas are treated, and you will be monitored as the anaesthesia wears off.

Recovery

Bruising and swelling are expected and settle over weeks; the garment is worn as directed to support healing tissues. Light walking is encouraged early, while strenuous exercise waits for medical clearance at follow-up. Numbness or firmness in treated areas usually softens with time. Because swelling masks early results, the final shape is judged over months, not days — patience is part of the process.

Risks and limitations — stated honestly

Every procedure carries trade-offs. Before you decide, Dr Ashok Reddy will discuss these with you directly:

  • Contour irregularities: uneven fat removal can leave waviness, dents, or asymmetry. Careful technique reduces this risk, but minor touch-ups are sometimes discussed after full healing.
  • Fluid accumulation and swelling: treated areas swell significantly, and fluid can occasionally collect beneath the skin. Compression garments and follow-up monitoring help manage this phase.
  • Changes in skin sensation: numbness, tingling, or firmness in treated areas is common and usually temporary, though altered sensation can persist longer in some patients.
  • Loose skin after fat removal: skin does not always retract fully, especially where elasticity is poor. This limitation is assessed honestly at consultation before any decision.

FAQ

Common questions

Is liposuction a weight-loss treatment?

No. Liposuction is designed for body contouring — reshaping localised fat pockets in people at or near a stable weight. It does not treat obesity, and removing large volumes is neither safe nor effective for weight loss. If significant weight change is your goal, Dr Reddy will discuss medical weight management first and revisit surgery once your weight is stable.

How much fat can be removed safely?

Safety limits how much fat is removed in a single session, and the right amount depends on your body, health, and the areas treated. Dr Reddy plans conservatively, because aggressive removal increases risks and often worsens the aesthetic result. If your goals need more than one session can safely deliver, staging the treatment is discussed openly.

What happens to loose skin after liposuction?

Skin with good elasticity retracts over the new contour, but loose or stretched skin may remain lax after fat removal. This is assessed at consultation by examining your skin quality. Where significant looseness exists, Dr Reddy may recommend an excision procedure such as a tummy tuck instead of — or alongside — liposuction, and will explain why honestly.

When can I return to work after liposuction?

It varies with the extent of treatment and the nature of your work. Desk-based work is often possible within days, while physically demanding jobs need longer. Swelling and soreness guide the pace, and Dr Reddy gives individual clearance at follow-up visits rather than a fixed date, since healing differs from person to person.

Will the fat come back in other areas?

Fat cells removed by liposuction do not return to the treated area. However, the remaining fat cells throughout your body can still enlarge if you gain weight, which may change your overall shape. Maintaining a stable weight through diet and activity is what preserves the contour achieved by surgery.
