Facelift (Rhytidectomy) in Karimnagar

A facelift does not aim to make you look like someone else — it aims to make you look like yourself on a well-rested day. At AR Plastic Surgery, Dr Ashok Reddy performs facelift surgery with an emphasis on natural, unoperated results.

Overview

A facelift addresses the visible signs of facial ageing: sagging cheeks, jowls, deep folds around the mouth, and loose neck skin. Modern technique repositions the deeper supporting tissues of the face rather than simply pulling skin tight, which is what avoids the stretched, windswept look of older methods. Excess skin is then redraped gently. The neck is very often addressed in the same operation. The overall effect should read as vitality rather than surgery.

It suits adults — typically from their forties onward — with noticeable laxity of the lower face and neck who are in good health and hold realistic expectations. Good candidates understand that a facelift refreshes rather than transforms, and that it does not change the fundamental structure of the face. Good skin quality and bone structure support better outcomes. Patients who are bothered by what they see daily, rather than chasing an ideal, tend to be happiest with the outcome.

Consultation involves assessing skin elasticity, the degree of sagging, facial volume loss, and medical fitness for anaesthesia. Dr Reddy discusses what bothers you most and whether a full facelift, a limited lift, or a non-surgical approach is the right choice. If your ageing is mild enough that surgery's trade-offs outweigh its benefits, or if expectations are unrealistic, he will counsel against operating. Your hairline, ear anatomy, and skin thickness are all examined, since they influence incision planning.

Honest expectations: a facelift turns back the clock but does not stop it — ageing continues, and results gradually soften over years. Scars are placed around the ears and into the hairline to hide discreetly, fading with time. Numbness and tightness are normal early on. The goal is that friends notice you look rested, not that you had surgery. Sun protection and a stable, healthy lifestyle help you enjoy the improvement for as long as possible. Results vary from person to person.

Our approach

Dr Reddy's technique focuses on the deeper layers: lifting and securing the supporting tissues gives longevity that skin-only tightening cannot, while the skin itself is redraped without tension. Vector of lift, incision placement, and attention to the hairline and ear contours are all planned to keep the result natural. The neck, jawline, and cheeks are treated as one aesthetic unit.

Restraint is the guiding principle. He will discourage over-aggressive lifting that would look operated, and he declines surgery when the motivation is a life event rather than a settled wish. The operation is performed under anaesthesia with careful bleeding control, and aftercare is structured — because in facelift surgery, how the first weeks go shapes the final result.

What to expect

Consultation

You will talk about what you see in the mirror that troubles you — jowls, neck laxity, tired appearance — and what you hope to change. Dr Reddy assesses your skin, facial structure, and medical fitness, and explains which technique suits your degree of ageing. You will see where scars will lie, discuss realistic longevity, and hear honestly whether surgery is advisable now or worth postponing.

The procedure

A facelift is performed under anaesthesia. Incisions are placed around the ears and into the hairline; deeper tissues are lifted and secured, excess skin is trimmed, and everything is closed with fine sutures. The neck is usually addressed through the same incisions. The operation takes several hours, and you are monitored closely as you emerge from anaesthesia. Dressings and gentle drains support the tissues during the first day, and you will be shown how to rest comfortably.

Recovery

Bruising and swelling peak in the first days and then settle over weeks; most people feel presentable within a few weeks, though subtle swelling lingers longer. Head elevation, cold compresses, and rest support healing. Strenuous activity waits for clearance. Numbness around the ears is common and fades gradually. Follow-up visits track healing through each stage. Stitches are removed in stages, and scar care guidance begins once healing allows.

Risks and limitations — stated honestly

Every procedure carries trade-offs. Before you decide, Dr Ashok Reddy will discuss these with you directly:

  • Haematoma: bleeding beneath the skin is the most common significant complication, occasionally requiring a return to theatre for drainage. Careful technique and blood pressure control reduce this risk.
  • Nerve injury: branches of the facial nerve lie close to the surgical plane, and temporary weakness of part of the face can occur. Lasting weakness is uncommon but is discussed honestly beforehand.
  • Scarring and hairline changes: scars are designed to hide, but healing varies, and the hairline or earlobe position can shift subtly. Scar care guidance is provided during follow-up.
  • Unsatisfactory or short-lived result: asymmetry, residual laxity, or faster-than-hoped softening of the result can occur. Realistic expectations and honest consultation minimise disappointment.

FAQ

Common questions

At what age should I consider a facelift?

There is no right age — the indication is the degree of laxity, not the birthday. Some patients benefit in their forties, others not until their sixties. What matters is visible sagging that bothers you, good health, and realistic expectations. Dr Reddy assesses your face, not your age, and will advise waiting if surgery is premature.

How long do facelift results last?

A facelift resets the clock but does not stop it; ageing continues from the new starting point. Many patients enjoy the improvement for many years, with gradual softening over time. Longevity depends on technique, skin quality, sun exposure, and lifestyle. Dr Reddy discusses realistic expectations for duration rather than promising a fixed number of years.

Will I look 'done' or unnatural?

Not with a well-planned operation. The unnatural look comes from skin pulled tight without addressing deeper tissues, or from over-aggressive lifting. Dr Reddy's approach repositions supporting structures and redrapes skin without tension, aiming for a rested, natural appearance — the kind where people notice you look well, not that you had surgery.

Does a facelift include the neck?

Very often, yes. The face and neck age together, and treating the face while ignoring a sagging neck gives an incomplete result. Neck contouring — addressing loose skin and muscle bands — is usually integrated into the facelift plan through the same incisions. Dr Reddy assesses both areas together at consultation.

Are there non-surgical alternatives?

For mild laxity, options like fillers, skin tightening treatments, or thread procedures may offer modest, temporary improvement — and Dr Reddy offers several of these himself. But no non-surgical treatment replicates what a facelift does for significant sagging. He will tell you honestly when a lesser treatment suffices and when only surgery will meet your goals.
